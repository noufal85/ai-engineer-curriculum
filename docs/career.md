# Career opportunities

Use this page to connect the curriculum to real **AI Engineer** and
**Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE)** roles. Every opening links back to its
LinkedIn source so you can confirm that it is still active, review the full
requirements, and apply from the original listing.

**Last reviewed: August 10, 2026**

!!! warning "Job listings change quickly"
    LinkedIn postings can close, move, or be reposted without notice. Treat the
    table as a discovery queue, not a guarantee that a role is still open.
    Verify the posting on LinkedIn before spending time on an application.

## Application tracker

Copy this row for each role you decide to pursue. Keep the LinkedIn URL even if
the company redirects you to a separate applicant-tracking system.

| Company | Role | LinkedIn URL | Fit notes | Status | Next action | Follow-up date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example Co. | AI Engineer | `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/...` | Strong RAG and evals fit; need stronger Kubernetes evidence | Saved | Tailor project bullets | YYYY-MM-DD |

Suggested statuses: **Saved → Researching → Applied → Interviewing → Closed**.

## Weekly career loop

1. Open all four live searches and save promising roles.
2. Check each role against the curriculum signals in the table.
3. Add the direct LinkedIn URL to your application tracker.
4. Tailor two or three portfolio bullets to the role's actual requirements.
5. Recheck saved listings and follow-ups once a week.
